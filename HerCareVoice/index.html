Unanswered After-Hours Calls

Implementation Flow

How It Works

Four simple, automated steps that connect patients with your care providers seamlessly.

01

Patient Calls Clinic Number

Your existing clinic phone number automatically forwards unanswered or after-hour calls to Maya, or patients dial your dedicated HerCare line directly. There are no wait times or queues.

02

Maya Answers Instantly

With clinical empathy, Maya greets the caller, gathers patient information (including full name, date of birth, and email), and details the specific women's health service they are seeking.

03

Checks Availability & Books

Integrating directly with Cal.com and Google Calendar, Maya reads real-time provider openings, presents available slots, and books the appointment instantly.

04

Logs Post-Call Structured Data

A comprehensive call summary, booking status, and patient demographics are automatically recorded to Google Sheets and synchronized with your EMR system.
